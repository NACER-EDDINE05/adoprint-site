# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses and individuals in Morocco who want to discover and order printed products.

## Product Purpose

ADO PRINT is an online print storefront for browsing a product catalog, configuring available product options and quantities, seeing prices, requesting quotes, adding products to a cart, and submitting orders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Laravel application and catalog are authoritative for products, images, descriptions, prices, and ordering behavior. The public storefront may be redesigned; Laravel pricing, cart, quote, checkout, database, and admin behavior must remain intact. The user plans to manage catalog and marketing content through an admin dashboard in the future; this work must not imply that new editing capabilities exist. Sample content may be used to demonstrate editable fields, but fabricated reviews, customer counts, performance statistics, and service guarantees must not be presented as real facts.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the ADO PRINT name and supplied official logo. Use blue, yellow, and white for the redesigned storefront. The user wants the public storefront to closely follow the visual composition, page structure, and interaction style of https://www.24hprint.ma/ while retaining ADO PRINT's own catalog and brand identity.

## Evidence on Hand

The Laravel application, existing ADO PRINT catalog and product images, configured company settings, product pricing grids, cart and quote flows are the source of truth. The 24hprint.ma website is a visual reference, not a source of ADO PRINT product, price, contact, customer, or service claims.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors browse real ADO PRINT products and reach their configured ordering flows quickly.
- Preserve existing catalog and Laravel business logic.
- Keep brand, product, and company information editable through existing mechanisms where available.
- Clearly distinguish illustrative samples from verified commercial facts.
